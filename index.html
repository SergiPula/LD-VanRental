      Questions

      Do I pay when I send the request?

      No. A request is not a payment. We contact you first to confirm availability and the details.

      How are rental days counted?

      Both the pickup day and the return day count. For example, 10 June to 12 June is 3 days.

      Which area do you cover?

      Pickup and return addresses are in the Athens area. Need something else? Message us and we’ll see what’s possible.

      What do I need at pickup?

      A valid driver’s license. We check it in person, so you don’t upload anything online.

      Can I change or cancel a request?

      Yes, just contact us by WhatsApp or email with your name and dates.
